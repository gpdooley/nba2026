      📜 Pool Rules & Scoring

      1. Objective (Off-Points Scoring)

      Predict the final 1-to-15 standings for both the Eastern and Western conferences. Your score is the total number of spots your predicted positions are off from the actual live NBA standings across all 30 teams. Lower total points is better!

      2. Scoring Example

      If you predict the Celtics to finish #1 in the East and they finish #3, you receive 2 off-points for that pick. A perfect exact-match pick earns 0 off-points.

      3. Combined Tie-Breaker Logic

      If two participants tie on total off-points, ties are broken rank-by-rank from 1st down to 15th:

      • Step 1: Sum the off-points for both 1st place picks (East #1 + West #1). Whoever has fewer combined off-points at #1 takes the higher leaderboard spot.
      • Step 2: If still tied, step down sequentially to #2 (East #2 + West #2), then #3, and so on.
      • Step 3: If combined error at a rank is equal, the single best pick at that rank breaks the tie before moving to the next rank down.
      4. Submissions & Edits

      You may re-submit your predictions at any time before the deadline using the exact same name. The system automatically keeps only your most recent entry.
